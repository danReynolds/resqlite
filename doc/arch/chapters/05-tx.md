Transactions map nesting onto savepoints and hold the writer mutex for the duration of the body. The measured truth of this subsystem is humbling: the per-statement isolate round-trip dominates everything else, and most clever control-path work dissolves against that floor.

## What the floor permits

Cached control statements removed real prepare/finalize work [[101.1]], but savepoint string costs sit below the decision threshold even at absurd nesting depths [[111.1]], name compression failed its representative workload [[189.1]], and both lazy savepoint materialization and statement fusion regress the shapes that matter [[212.1]]. Even round-trip coalescing inside a transaction — mechanically sound — was rejected because the pattern it accelerates has no idiomatic home in the API: bulk atomic writes belong on executeBatch [[213.1]].
