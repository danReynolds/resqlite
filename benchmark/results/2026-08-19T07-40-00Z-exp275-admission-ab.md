# Exp 275 — cost-aware reader-pool admission, focused A/B receipt

Apple M1 Pro (10 cores, reader pool = 4), macOS 26.2 (Darwin 25.2.0), Dart 3.12.x.
Three native-asset-aware AOT CLI bundles built with `dart build cli` from one
harness source (`benchmark/experiments/reader_admission_priority.dart`):

- **base** — `origin/main` @ f44c2c4.
- **candA** — priority waiter queues only (`reserveLastWorker = false`).
- **candB** — priority waiter queues + last-free-worker reservation.

Every lane runs in its own process (`--lane=`), 41 samples after 10 warmup
samples. Two collections, each with an order-flipped pair: pass 1 runs
base -> candA -> candB, pass 2 runs candB -> candA -> base. Percentages are
candidate against base within the same pass. Verdicts are
`benchmark/ab_drift_check.dart`'s, computed per collection.

## Incidence probe

One `benchmark/run_release.dart --repeat=1` pass over the whole release suite,
with a temporary unguarded counter in `_dispatch` (since removed). Totals are
cumulative across every `Database` the suite opens, read at each `close()`:

| dispatches | cheap | cheap that parked | cheap that parked behind a costly waiter |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 335,221 | 325,909 (97.2%) | 312 (0.096% of cheap) | **0** |

The probe build had the reservation ON, which parks costly requests *more*
often than main does, so the zero is an upper bound measured against the
condition most likely to produce a non-zero.

`bulk4-cold` is the one lane whose reading is not stable: it reproduced in
collection 1 for both arms and went neutral (candA) or opposite-signed (candB)
in collection 2. Nothing in either arm touches it — four concurrent large reads
on a four-worker pool never park, and the lane exists precisely to confirm the
reservation stays disarmed without cheap traffic, which the sign flip confirms
it does. Read it as process-level noise on a ~290 us sample, not as an effect.
