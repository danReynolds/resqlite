# exp225 base64 12-bit LUT — focused A/B

Three order-flipped passes of
`benchmark/experiments/select_bytes_blob_base64.dart` on the same worktree
(candidate = `native/resqlite.c` with the 12-bit pair LUT; baseline =
`git stash`ed to origin/main). Medians are microseconds per `selectBytes()`
query. Negative = candidate faster.

## Summary

| Lane | ΔP1 | ΔP2 | ΔP3 | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 3B × 8 (guard) | -0.9% | -0.9% | -0.5% | flat, no regression |
| 3B × 20 (guard) | +3.8% | -1.6% | -1.3% | sign reversal / noise |
| 16B × 8 | -8.4% | -1.2% | -6.0% | candidate-faster 2/3 |
| **128B × 4** | **-31.2%** | **-17.4%** | **-26.4%** | reproduced ≥17% |
| **4KB × 2** | **-31.4%** | **-26.5%** | **-29.1%** | reproduced ≥26% |
| Mixed | -3.8% | -1.2% | +2.5% | small movement either direction |

The 4KB and 128B lanes clear the acceptance gate (≥3% faster reproduced
across the order flip, same direction) and the 3B tiny-cell guards stay flat.
