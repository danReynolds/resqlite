# Exp 218 - 8x base64 loop unroll for selectBytes BLOBs

Focused `benchmark/experiments/select_bytes_blob_base64.dart` A/B against
`origin/main` at `a217824`. Candidate widens exp 216's native
`json_write_base64` payload-loop unroll from four 3-byte groups per trip to
eight groups per trip. JSON quote framing, base64 table, padding, encoded
length, and output bytes are unchanged.

Harness reports median microseconds per `selectBytes()` query. Lower is
better.

## Interpretation

The 8x unroll did not clear the exp 216 acceptance gate. The 128B medium-BLOB
lane stayed candidate-faster in all four passes (-14.0%, -5.8%, -5.8%,
-3.4%), which suggests the wider loop can reduce some loop-control work.
However, the 4KB throughput gate was mixed: two passes were clearly faster
(-10.0%, -13.6%), while the two order-flipped passes were neutral/slower
(+2.0%, +0.8%). Tiny and mixed guard lanes also lost direction in the last
pair.

That makes the result below the merge bar. Exp 216's four-triplet scalar
unroll remains the right portable baseline; simply widening the unroll to eight
triplets adds code shape without a reproduced payload-throughput win.
